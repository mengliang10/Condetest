The idea behind the journey

Not one Silk Road. Many worlds.

Southern Xinjiang is often described as a place on the Silk Road. But a route on a map reveals only part of the story. Across these ten days, the landscape shifts from the high Pamirs to the Taklamakan's desert edge, and the cultural record shifts with it: mountain communities, old trading towns, Islamic architecture and Buddhist cave art.

This is the value of the journey's sequence. Kashgar introduces a living city; the Pamirs widen the horizon; Yarkant and Hotan recall exchange along the southern routes; Kuqa reveals older Buddhist traditions. Tianchi closes the journey in a landscape entirely different from where it began.

Rather than claiming access unavailable elsewhere, this concept emphasises what a well-designed private journey can offer: a coherent route, time with a local guide, the flexibility to respond to interests and the logistical work needed to connect these distant places.

A journey in three movements
First the mountains. Then the desert. Finally, the traces of cultures that met between them.

The journey in detail

Ten days, thoughtfully connected.

This day-by-day route preserves the published Intriq itinerary. Descriptions have been rewritten for clarity and narrative coherence, without adding unverified private access or activities.

01
Kashgar

The threshold of Central Asia

Arrive in Kashgar, meet your local guide and arrange the required border permit. Settle into the Radisson Blu Hotel Kashgar. The first evening is deliberately unhurried: an opportunity to orient yourself before the mountain journey.

OVERNIGHT · Radisson Blu Hotel Kashgar
02
Kashgar → Tashkurgan

Into the Pamirs

Travel approximately 300 km / six hours by 4×4 Toyota Prado convoy. The route climbs toward the Pamir Plateau, past White Sand Lake and Karakul Lake, where a picnic is planned. Yak hot pot is scheduled for dinner.

OVERNIGHT · Panoramic Star Hotel
03
Tashkurgan → Kashgar

Roads through a mountain crossroads

Begin with sunrise at Golden Grass Beach and visit Stone City. Continue along the winding Panlong road and Tasha route, passing near the Wakhan Corridor. The itinerary includes lunch in a Tajik home and participation in food preparation. Apricot blossom viewing is seasonal and not guaranteed.

OVERNIGHT · Radisson Blu Hotel Kashgar
04
Kashgar

A city of living crafts

Explore Kashgar's old-town streets, traditional craft practices, Id Kah Mosque and the Abakh Khoja mausoleum. The livestock market operates on Sundays only; the market visit depends on the departure date. Allow the guide to explain the city's layered history rather than reducing it to a checklist.

OVERNIGHT · Radisson Blu Hotel Kashgar
05
Kashgar → Yarkant → Hotan

Cities that connected continents

Drive approximately 500 km / 5.5 hours according to the source itinerary. Stop in Yarkant to see the Altun Mosque complex, the tomb of musician and queen Amannisa Khan, and the old town, before continuing to Hotan.

OVERNIGHT · Hotan Silk Road Welcome Hotel
06
Hotan → Kuqa

The desert crossing

A long overland day, approximately 650 km / eight hours, across the Taklamakan Desert Highway. Look for changing desert landscapes, the Tarim River corridor and poplar forests. The long transfer is part of the geography of this journey, not a hidden footnote.

OVERNIGHT · Kuqa Tian Yuan International Hotel
07
Kuqa

Where Buddhist art met the Silk Road

Visit the Kizil Caves, known for Buddhist rock-cut art reflecting cultural exchanges along ancient trade routes. Continue to Kuqa's historic quarter, with the King's Mansion possible if time allows. Cave access and photography restrictions should be reconfirmed.

OVERNIGHT · Kuqa Tian Yuan International Hotel
08
Kuqa → Urumqi

From caravan routes to the modern city

Fly from Kuqa to Urumqi. The original itinerary lists flight CZ6872 at 09:55–11:00, which must be reconfirmed for the travel date. Depending on arrival time, visit the Xinjiang Regional Museum and its archaeological collections.

OVERNIGHT · Conrad Urumqi
09
Urumqi

A final landscape: Tianchi Lake

Spend the day at Tianchi, the alpine lake beneath the Tianshan range, with time for walking and enjoying the landscape. The quieter final chapter contrasts with the cities, desert and mountain roads of the preceding days.

OVERNIGHT · Conrad Urumqi
10
Urumqi

The journey continues

Breakfast and private transfer to the airport for departure. The original itinerary ends here; any onward extension would require a separate proposal.
